# Submission descriptions

## Version 1

Saathi helps a patient or ASHA report a missed medicine refill in Hindi and follow it until the patient receives the medicine.  
Staff verify stock, an officer reviews a proposed transfer, and the case history records every step. The current demo uses synthetic data.

## Version 2

When Ramesh cannot get metformin at his health centre, Saathi turns his report into a trackable case.  
It connects pharmacist verification, district stock visibility, an approved transfer, and patient confirmation. The prototype uses synthetic records.

## Version 3

Saathi is a Hindi-first refill-resolution prototype for primary health centres.  
It combines patient reports, stock and demand signals, and human-approved transfer drafts so a reported shortage can be followed to closure. Its evaluation and demo data are synthetic.
