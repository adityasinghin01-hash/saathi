- **₹ cost per district, per patient, or national rollout:** no priced staffing, hosting, integration, device, support and training model exists in the supplied files.
- **National ASHA smartphone access rate** and **national share of PHCs using paper registers:** [PLAN §13](/Users/aditya/dev/c4c2-ideas/PLAN.md) explicitly leaves these unconfirmed.
- **37.9% catastrophic spending as a stockout consequence:** the PLAN cites a diabetes-care spending analysis, but it does not establish stockouts as the cause; do not use for this deck's supply claim.
- **23 crore Scan & Share tokens; Indian thali photo accuracy; any field reduction in stockouts, missed doses or private spending:** unverified or unmeasured in [PLAN §13](/Users/aditya/dev/c4c2-ideas/PLAN.md).
