Saathi · साथी is a live synthetic prototype for patients with diabetes or BP, ASHA workers, and government PHCs.
A missed refill reported by Hindi voice goes to a pharmacist check, an AI-drafted stock transfer approved by an officer, a “medicine arrived” alert, and case closure after hand-over.

**Variant 1**
Patients with diabetes or BP and ASHA workers report missed refills to government PHCs by Hindi voice.
In this live synthetic prototype, a pharmacist checks stock, an officer approves an AI-drafted transfer, a “medicine arrived” alert appears, and the case closes after the patient gets medicine.
